By creating different textures, the fabric gives the illusion of many different colors and shades depending on the yarns used and their placements.

In this development I found it was best to use a shade gradient of colors from light, medium-light, medium-dark and dark. This could be executed with tonally similar yarns or different tones. This created the greatest range and variance in color + texture options.
