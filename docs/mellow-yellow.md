An initial exploration of a sleeveless sweater with a very long neck before I knew it would become primary inspiration for my senior collection.

This sweater is made out of a slubby 100% cotton yarn. You can see how the yarn took up the direct dye after two dye baths, giving it rich heather look. First appearing a bright canary yellow, then, after the second dye bath, it pulled out some rich golden tones.

Here I also experimented with creating different knit-purl structures on the Shima Seiki Wholegarment machine.

You can also observe how different lighting conditions affect the appearance of the color.
